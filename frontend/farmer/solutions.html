Farmer · Kallur Village · 12 Mar 2026

Spray neem oil solution — 5ml per 1 litre of water. Apply every 3 days for 2 weeks. Best applied in early morning or late evening to avoid leaf burn.

Maintain proper plant spacing. Avoid over-watering. Ensure good drainage channels in the field.

Mani Krishnan
Farmer · Bhavanisagar · 13 Mar 2026

Apply Tricyclazole fungicide (0.6g/L) or Carbendazim (1g/L) dissolved in water. Spray thoroughly on affected plants including the underside of leaves.

Wear protective gloves and face mask while spraying. Keep children and livestock away for 24 hours. Avoid spraying near water bodies.

👩
Lakshmi Devi
Farmer · Sathyamangalam · 14 Mar 2026

Remove and destroy all visibly infected plant parts immediately. Apply potassium (K) fertiliser to boost plant immunity. Reduce nitrogen application temporarily.

Do not flood the field during treatment period. Switch to blast-resistant paddy varieties (CO 51, ADT 45) for next season.
